VI. Notes

The same Ariadne World as the tract, on another anatomy (TractNdAnatomy::fart_v0): a self-oscillating valve (a mass on a tension spring with contact; a jet with viscous and Bernoulli loss) for a sphincter, a canal flaring into a cleft whose 24 modes are recomputed whenever the cheeks move, POC-3's air (exact seals, the jet's separation loss, a turbulent dipole), and one ledger.

Digesting pays the World's reservoir (its work_input, booked as source work); releasing spends it, and below 40% the push sags by the valve's own law: nothing scripts the end. The valve does not land where it is told: it runs sharp of the commanded tension by up to a few semitones, which is why the exercises score what the World measured.

Invented: every anatomy number, the provisions, the scores, the Auditor's opinions. The canal is 20 cm, longer than any body's, because the World's 24 modes must fit in its band.

Not modeled: the cheeks flapping in the flow, a sphincter as a ring, three-dimensional air, and smell.
