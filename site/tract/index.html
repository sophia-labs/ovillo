THE BOOKS (JOULES, SINCE START)

WHAT THIS IS

A vocal tract made of an Ariadne World, after Neil Thapen's Pink Trombone (2017), written independently. Nothing here plays a recording or a stored glottal pulse: lung pressure pushes air through two folds, the folds start to flap on their own, and the flapping air rings in a tube shaped by your tongue, lips and velum. Every joule is on the books: what the breathing muscles and the orbs put in, what moving the walls cost, what was lost to the walls, the jet and the folds, and what left through the lips and nostrils. The residual is printed above.

PHYSICAL (ONE ARIADNE WORLD)

  • The tract is one body of a mus_ariadne::World, the same runtime the Ariadne guitar plays in, with one ledger. Its configuration is a point in fourteen dimensions: tongue index, tongue diameter, lips, velum, and the place and degree of five constrictions.
  • At every configuration the World computes the body's 24 lowest modes from the shape of the air: an oral tube of 44 compressible sections and a nasal branch of 31, joined by inertive air columns (a discretized Webster horn, the lips and nostrils opening to the room). Moving the shape holds the air's excess volume and momentum fixed and re-projects them onto the new modes; the energy that changes is booked as control work, and what the 24 modes cannot hold is booked as disposal.
  • The glottis self-oscillates: a fold mass on a tension spring with collision, pushed by the air in the slit, fed by a finite lung reservoir the breathing muscles refill. Its jet enters the first section with exactly the work it does there. Pitch is the fold tension; the World decides the actual F0.
  • Air beyond linear acoustics: the narrowest column carries a jet that loses its dynamic pressure (a free jet), and a turbulent force a few columns downstream returns a share of that loss as sound (0.4% at Mach 0.1, growing as Mach³, switched on by Reynolds number), paid out of the jet's loss. A column whose area falls below GARGANTA's 0.025 cm² floor follows it down to a seal; closure is a configuration like any other, so pressure builds behind a stop from the lungs and the release spends it.
  • Touch the cross-section to place constrictions; vibrato and wobble retune the fold, booked; an orb knocks the air where it lands, its energy booked as source work.

APPROXIMATED, AND WHY

  • Wall loss and radiation are diagonal in the modes (exact only for uniform loss). Once the air flows they act on momentum and pressure respectively so a stop's pressure does not leak; a steady flow is then over-charged by the frequency-independent wall loss, and the booked radiation also drains static pressure that real radiation would not.
  • One jet (the narrowest column), quasi-steady, with no pressure recovery; turbulence is white noise shaped only by the tract; there is no glottal aspiration noise (the aspiration control is accepted and does nothing yet); an orb does not dent the wall.
  • The shape is moved in steps (every 32 samples while it travels, every 128 in light mode), not continuously; the steps cost a few dB above 6 kHz during fast gestures. Light mode is the same World, moved less often.
  • One fold mass with a lumped mucosal delay. The air's work on the folds is booked to the lungs. Vowel presets are a fit in this World to GARGANTA's rung-1 vowel bodies (design profiles, not measurements); /u/ needs a small velopharyngeal opening.
  • The pictures: geometry follows the World every frame (areas, fold gap, wall pressure). The idle sway of the camera, the sheen and the orb splashes are decoration.
