# Generation 3 next tranche

## Objective

Determine whether a learned constitutive body supplies capabilities or
inductive biases beyond ordinary recurrent vectors, and which parts of the
constitution are causally load-bearing.

## Baselines

Every promoted task includes:

- affine history features;
- GRU;
- selective state-space model;
- Neural CDE or path-signature model;
- isotropic neural cellular automaton;
- Neural Wave Machine;
- fixed random local reservoir plus trained readout;
- wave-only local medium;
- evidence-only local medium;
- full Generation 3 medium.

Match and report separately:

- trainable parameters;
- active parameters per step;
- live recurrent state;
- sequence FLOPs;
- observer capacity;
- unique data;
- optimization steps.

## Experiment G3.1 — causal sector anatomy

Tasks:

- equal-multiset path order;
- closed-loop orientation;
- positive filtering and majority;
- mixed path/filter switching.

Interventions:

- zero signed sector;
- zero evidence sector;
- reservoir depletion;
- edge deletion;
- edge-order permutation;
- observer weakening;
- equal-norm state and parameter shams.

Primary result:

A sector or edge is causal only when its targeted removal selectively damages
the capability predicted by the constitutive interpretation.

## Experiment G3.2 — damage and developmental repair

Compare:

- static learned constitution;
- training with random edge availability;
- local developmental repair rule;
- NCA repair baseline;
- global optimizer repair ceiling.

Damage varies by location, duration, and timing. State is never reset.

Primary outcomes:

- immediate capability deficit;
- state-storage loss;
- time to recovery;
- retained unrelated capability;
- source and configuration work;
- recovery after novel damage patterns.

## Experiment G3.3 — path complexity and local commutator depth

Generate controlled paths with known truncated-signature requirements. Vary:

- signature/log-signature order;
- graph diameter;
- edge degree;
- number of ordered transport substeps;
- signed-state dimension.

Test whether the minimum local ordered depth tracks the algebraic path
complexity better than raw parameter count.

## Experiment G3.4 — predictive realization compiler

Generate observable processes with independent:

```text
predictive rank       2–128
Hankel condition      1–10^6
mixing time           logarithmic ladder
spectral profile      flat / geometric / weak mode
transport category    projective / orthogonal / affine / mixed
```

Compare direct neural training, spectral initialization, positive factorization,
and local-medium compilation. Measure future-test error, captured predictive
spectral mass, compiler size, observer weakness, and work closure.

## Experiment G3.5 — refinement and remeshing

This experiment cannot begin until an identity-changing state-transfer IR is
implemented.

Separate:

- fixed-domain refinement;
- domain growth;
- constitutive-law capacity;
- live-state remeshing.

Require three-level self-convergence, work closure, state-transport receipts,
orientation/permutation tests, and explicit refusal outside the valid domain.

## Experiment G3.6 — persistent natural sequences

Only mechanisms clearing G3.1–G3.4 graduate.

Use residual byte/character prediction after a strong local baseline and cross:

- persistent state with truncated gradients;
- explicit state reset;
- gradient detach without reset;
- shuffled chunks;
- local damage;
- observer-capacity controls;
- matched SSM and Neural CDE baselines.

A natural-data gain without the controlled causal signature counts as an
architecture result, not confirmation of the constitutive mechanism.

## Scale axes

Do not collapse these into one variable:

```text
sites                  4, 16, 64, 256, 1024, 4096
local degree           2, 4, 8, 16
sequence length         8 through 65,536
predictive rank         2 through 128
observer dimension      weak through diagnostic ceiling
constitutive capacity   fixed law through learned local law
material resolution     only after refinement semantics exist
training compute        data, updates, and FLOPs reported separately
```
