# Diary: u03-m58 (sections 06-m58, 07-m74)

Oboe, violas, bassoons and low strings, at 65 bpm. Parts v1 to v6 are the oboe, viola, fagotto I, celli, fagotto II and C. bassi of 06-m58 (no cannon line in the source for these bars, so `cannon.notes` stays rests). 07-m74 is nine beats for four low parts: fagotto I, celli, C. bassi and fagotto II as v1 to v4; v5 and v6 are tacet.

## Pass 1: the physics, and the targets

**What I meant.** Each part plays its source line at sounding pitch. The valve cannot sound below about 46 Hz or above 292 Hz (the probe saturates at D4), so every note is folded into 48 to 285 Hz, to the octave nearest a centre per voice: oboe 210, viola 165, fagotto I 125, celli 110, fagotto II 80, C. bassi 68 Hz. That keeps the voices in register order on average. The baseline's C#5 in the viola and D4-range oboe notes were above the valve's reach.

**What I set.** push 0.7, cheeks 0.4, tongue on, tone 0.4.

**What the probe said.** `probe --push 0.7 --cheeks 0.4 --tone 0.75` (default tone) lands 195 to 240 cents sharp of tension across 84 to 160 Hz. At tone 0.2 nothing swings from 30 to 300 Hz. At tone 0.4, a 58-step sweep from 40 to 330 Hz gave: no swing below about 46 Hz (tension 48.1 gave 45.6 Hz, -93 cents); tension 69.3 gave 72.1 Hz (+68); 85.4 gave 90.6 Hz (+101); 105.3 gave 111.9 Hz (+104); 129.9 gave 135.7 Hz (+77); 160.1 gave 163.3 Hz (+34); 197.4 gave 201 Hz (+31); 243.3 gave 242.4 Hz (-6); 300 gave 292.4 Hz (saturated). Sharpness 9 to 10 between 60 and 110 Hz and 4 to 5 above 180 Hz. Tone 0.4 is the lowest tone that swings through the bass with usable tuning, so I kept it.

**How I commanded tensions.** I did not add the probe offset by hand. I inverted the measured curve (log-interpolated measured Hz against tension, 46 to 287 Hz), so each note's commanded tension is the one the probe says lands on its target. Tensions run 55.8 (A1) to 283.5 (C#4). The generator is at `scratchpad/gen/gen.py` and writes the parts.

## Pass 3: four beans in the pre-silence (06-m58, --no-mix)

**What the World measured.** Still dry. v1: 32/78 in tune, 46 silent, 31 dry, gas at end 0.006. v2: 30/71, 38 silent, 32 dry. v3: 21/81, 56 silent, 51 dry. v4: 17/77, 54 silent, 51 dry. v5: 17/77, 54 silent, 52 dry. v6: 12/73, 53 silent, 51 dry. Stomach left 0.0 J in all six.

**What I heard in them.** The stomach was empty at the end of every part, so the four beans were all paid out, and the reservoir still ran down through the middle of the part. My reading (an inference, not something the report states) is that a bean's gas arrives over its digestion time, so food fed up front runs out early and has to be spread out with the playing.

**What I changed.** I measured the draw from v6's reading. Its reservoir fell from about 0.9 to 0.25 of full in 4 s while sounding, about 0.25 W, so I needed roughly three to four beans digesting at once (0.075 W each, eight seconds).

## Retune (fart_v1)

**What the new instrument did to my parts.** The first run's tensions were inverted from the `fart_v0` probe. On `fart_v1` with each voice's own anatomy (v1 and v2 soprano, v3 and v4 alto, v5 and v6 bass), most notes landed sharp or flat by the amounts the new probe gives. Before the retune, 06-m58 had 265 of 455 sounding notes within 50 cents (v1 60/76, v2 51/71, v3 66/81, v4 46/77, v5 19/77, v6 23/73), with per-part mean errors of 33 to 63 cents. The bass was worst: v5 and v6 had their A2 notes near 104 Hz for a target of 110 Hz. No note was reported silent or started dry at this point (render 1, `silentNotes` 0 everywhere), but many were out of tune.

**What I measured first.** `probe --score scores/fart/1812 --voice vN --push 0.7 --cheeks 0.4 --tone 0.4` for each voice, 40 to 300 Hz, 130 steps, as JSON. The `--score` probe carries the house direction (jitter, rasp, vibrato) and matches the render: at 57 to 60 of the bass, `--anatomy bass` alone gave sharpness 10.2 and `--score` gave 8.5, so I used `--score`. Examples from the soprano probe: tension 153.3 gave 155.5 Hz; 220.0 gave 214.3 Hz; 270.5 gave 261 Hz. The bass: 106.8 gave 109.0 Hz; 124.6 gave 126.5 Hz. Above about 285 Hz the soprano plateaus at 276 to 288 Hz.

**What I set.** I kept push 0.7, cheeks 0.4, tone 0.4, tongue on, and the same feeding (`pre=4 eat=beans*5 digest=on` for v3 to v6 with the inline `eat=beans*3` or `*5` steps as before), so the beats and bar lines did not change. For every note I inverted the probe (monotone fit of log Hz against tension, interpolated) to get the tension that lands on the note's name, then rewrote its `@` value. Render 2 measured the result: 06-m58 v1 71/76 (one silent), v2 69/71, v3 78/81, v4 73/77, v5 70/77, v6 61/73. Mean errors fell to 13.7 to 26.4 cents for v2 to v5, and v6 stayed at 33.4.
