**What I set.** Probes at push 0.7, cheeks 0.4 (tensions 36 to 300 Hz):
- tone 0.75: the valve runs 150 to 240 cents sharp from about 70 to 180 Hz; no swing at 36 Hz.
- tone 0.4: about 70 to 110 cents sharp from 70 to 220 Hz; sharpness 9 to 10 in the 80 to 120 Hz band; too low a tension (42.4 Hz) gives 29.9 Hz.
