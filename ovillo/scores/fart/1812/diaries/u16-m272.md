## Pass 1: tuned hands, probed tensions, first feeding (render 1)

Corno III and Corno II carry the middle lines, Corno IV the lowest horn line. The bassi keeps its run of half-beat notes at the bottom of the texture, and the celli sits at its written pitch.

**Register decisions (changes from the baseline).**
- Corno II and Corno IV were baseline-shifted an octave down (78 to 104 Hz, under the bassi's own register). I put them back at sounding pitch (147 to 208 Hz), so the inner horns sit in register order: Corno I above Corno III above Corno II above Corno IV.
- Celli were baseline-shifted an octave down (unisons with the bassi). I put them at sounding pitch (86 to 289 Hz), so the octave between celli and bassi in the source survives.
