## Retune (fart_v1)

**What I meant.** The same music as before: the violins on the tune, the clarinets and the bassoon in their register order, the celli on the bass figures, the fagotto on the lower line. Only the instrument changed under me, so I kept every note, octave, beat and bar line and changed the tensions, the push on restarts, and the tone on the few notes that would not speak.

**What I did not measure first.** I rendered the parts as I received them only after I had already rewritten their tensions, so I have no tool measurement of the untouched parts on fart_v1. **The instrument (probes).** Each voice's own anatomy (`probe --score ... --voice vN`), at push 0.7 or 1.0 and cheeks 0.4, tone 0.4 and 0.75, tensions 48 to 300 Hz in 70 steps. I fitted a smoothed, monotone map from tension to measured pitch for each voice and setting (median residual 8.3 to 10.4 cents). Example, v1 at push 0.7, tone 0.4: a 60 Hz target needs tension 61.5, 100 Hz needs 96.6, 150 Hz needs 147.8, 250 Hz needs 258.1. **What I set.**
