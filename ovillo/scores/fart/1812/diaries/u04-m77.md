# Diary, u04-m77 (sections 08-m77 horns and basses, 09-m93 strings)

## Pass 0: what I heard in the source, and the instrument

The horns (08) carry the tune: Corno I and III share a line, Corno II and IV
share another, all in F with a fanfare-like figure of eighths and quarters.
Below them the celli and C. Basses hold Bb (a long tie chain). Section 09 is
a short string cadence: Violini I to B, Violini II to F#, Viole to Eb, and a
Bb in the celli and basses, twelve beats in all.

Probes at push 0.7, cheeks 0.4 (tone 0.4 and 0.75) set the register. The valve
is a continuous map from tension to pitch, not a set of locked modes, so I
inverted a dense sweep (25 to 300 Hz, 1500 steps) for each setting. The
ceiling is what matters: at tone 0.4 the highest pitch the valve will hold is
about 292.4 Hz; at tone 0.75 it is 305.7 Hz; at push 1.0 with tone 1.0 it is
323.4 Hz. Nothing I can set reaches F4 (349) or G4 (392), so horn I's F4 and
G4 cannot be played at their written octave on this gut.

I chose tone 0.4 (the valve is about 100 cents sharp in the 100 to 130 Hz
range and near true above 200 Hz, versus about 240 cents at tone 0.75), and
moved each horn line a whole octave down from the source. The baseline folded
notes one by one (Corno I had C4 at +0 next to F3 and Eb3 folded down), which
would have broken the melodic intervals; a consistent octave keeps the tune.
Basses and strings stay where the baseline put them.

How I retuned.
