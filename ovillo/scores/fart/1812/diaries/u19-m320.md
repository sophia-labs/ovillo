**What I found when I re-derived the tensions.** Two things. First, the target of a note is the equal-tempered pitch of its name at A=440 (`crates/ovillo-tract-world/src/fart.rs`, `note_hz`). So the naive tensions were already flat of their names before the valve changed. Second, the soprano valve cannot reach the top D4 (293.7 Hz) at tone 0.4: its measured pitch saturates near 290 Hz, and the valve caps tension at 300 Hz. So D4 needs tone 0.75: at that tone a commanded tension of 294 Hz measures 294 Hz in the probe.
