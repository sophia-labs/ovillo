# u07-m128 (sections 12-m128, Allegro giusto, continued)

Composer and player: the diary of unit u07. Six voices (v1 Violini I, v2 Violini II, v3 Trombe Eb I, v4 Clarinetto in Bb II, v5 Viole, v6 Celli) and the cannon, 64 beats (27.83 s at 138 bpm).

## Pass 1: first tensions, feeding beans*3, tongue on (render 1)

**What I meant.** Keep the top line of each part as the baseline wrote it, octave-moved into 36-300 Hz, and make every note land on its target. The tune should be where the ear expects it in the violins and trombe; the clarinet and viole inner voices stay in register order; the bass (celli) stays a bass. The piatti and gran cassa hit at bar 16 beat 2 is the cannon's one shot.

**What I set.** Inverted a tension table at push 0.7, cheeks 0.4 for each note (`probe` at tone 0.3 and 0.4; a 12-step sweep at tone 0.3 agreed with the 64-step table: 58 Hz commanded gave 56.9, 89.1 gave 91.6, 136.9 gave 137.2, 182.3 gave 181.7, 242.7 gave 237.8, 280 gave 270.8). Tone 0.3 below 215 Hz, tone 0.4 above. Notes under 56 Hz moved up an octave. Tongue on everywhere. Fed `pre=4 eat=beans*3 digest=on`.

**What the World measured (render 1, no mix).** In tune (within 50 cents) was 34/129, 37/135, 17/53, 15/91, 14/116, 19/100 for v1 to v6, which is 136/624. Started dry: 53, 60, 19, 33, 59, 37. Gas at end 0.01 in every voice. Clipped: 207, 196, 12, 72, 64, 98. Pops reached 92.7 dB.

**What I heard in them.** The reservoir ran out after about 14 s. The 1.5 J reservoir was drained by a sounding note at about 0.38 W gross (my estimate from the gas column: the drop from 1.0 to 0.703 over 1.85 s, plus the 0.225 W of three beans digesting), and three beans could not keep up. Every note after the first dry one is measured as no swing, and the in-tune count is mostly the 'no swing' notes that happen to be early. The tuning is not the problem here; the gas is.

**What I changed.** Feeding: beans*6 at pre and at bars 5, 10 and 14 (four batches of 3.6 J, 14.4 J total, digesting at 0.45 W each for 8 s). Slurs: tongue off except after a rest.

## Pass 2: beans*6 in four batches, slurred runs (render 2, no mix)

**What I set.** Feeding as above. `tongue=off` for runs, `tongue=on` only for the first note after a rest. Tone and tensions unchanged from pass 1.

**What the World measured.** In tune 106/129, 113/135, 41/53, 67/91, 78/116, 56/100, which is 461/624 (73.9%). Silent 12, 11, 9, 6, 8, 7 (53 in total). Started dry 0 everywhere. Gas at end 0.984, 0.988, 0.975, 0.985, 0.857, 0.968; stomach left 5.40, 6.07, 7.05, 4.13, 1.25, 2.09 J. Clipped 59, 55, 20, 30, 51, 30. Mean cents 135, 120, 376, 158, 178, 195.

**What I heard in them.** Feeding worked: no note started dry and every part finished with gas near full. The remaining loss has two shapes. (a) Notes that follow a rest and last 1/4 or 1/2 beat do not start: the per-note analysis shows 26 of 30 after-rest 1/2 onsets and 45 of 49 after-rest 1/4 onsets as no swing; 3 of 4 one-beat onsets after a rest sounded. (b) Slurred steps are lagged: a descending step measures sharp (median +20 to +26 cents for small and mid steps), an ascending step flat (median -15 to -26), and a jump of more than 4 semitones measures 130 cents sharp when descending. The sustained pitches of the tone 0.3 table are good (repeated notes measured 34/34 in tune). Clipping came from tongued attack pops at full reservoir.

**What I changed.** Onsets after a rest push 1.0 (probe at push 1.0 tone 0.3 gave the same tension-to-pitch shape within about 15 cents). A slur-lag compensation: lower the commanded tension for descents, raise it for ascents, and turn tongue on for jumps over 4 semitones.

## Pass 3: onset push and slur compensation, first attempt (render 3, no mix)

**What I set.** Onset push 1.0 (back to 0.7 after the note). Compensation: 18 and 21 cents down, 16 and 20 cents up for small and mid steps. Tongue on for jumps over 4 semitones.

**What the World measured.** In tune 102/129, 112/135, 47/53, 58/91, 56/116, 51/100, which is 426/624 (68.3%). Silent 9, 8, 2, 1, 3, 1 (24 in total; the 53 of pass 2 fell mostly because onsets at push 1.0 now sounded). Gas at end 0.984, 0.983, 0.958, 0.984, 0.522, 0.522. Clipped 81, 95, 25, 45, 69, 60. Some notes missed by an octave or more: C3 at -396 cents in v3, C2 at -1533 in v4, D2 at -1760 in v5.

**What I heard in them.** The push fix sounded the 1/2 onsets (after-rest 1/2 onsets: 31 of 32 sounding, median -36 cents in the table). The compensation made the slurs worse. I had the sign backwards: the descending errors are sharp, so a descent needs a lower tension, and I raised it. The jumps with tongue on were worse too: descending big jumps had median +154 cents, and the tongue clench eats time at a 1/4 beat.

**What I changed.** The compensation sign (descents lower, ascents raise). Tongue off for jumps (it was on in pass 3). Onsets after a rest tongue off, to give a 1/4 note its full sound time. Compensation for after-rest onsets +30 cents. Big jumps (over 4 semitones) get -60 cents down and +30 cents up.

## Review (second composer)

**What I found.** The first composer's feeding, tone and tension table were sound: no note started dry, no clipping, every part ends with gas. The report I started from (cached render 4) had 505/624 in tune, with these losses. (a) Onsets after a rest lasting 1/4 beat (0.11 s) were silent or far off (v1 to v4 reported 9, 10, 3, 2 silent; v5 and v6 had onsets at -1705, -1554, -1511 c). (b) Descents of more than 4 semitones sat 100 to 150 c sharp. (c) Several low bass onsets (Bb1, B1, C2) were hundreds of cents off. The melody (violins, trombe), the inner voices and the bass line are the right passage in the right register order; I kept all of that.

**What I changed, and why.**
1. Onsets: an after-rest 1/4 note now takes the 1/4 of rest before it and sounds as a 1/2 note starting a sixteenth earlier. Every later onset in the run is where it was, and the beat counts are unchanged. The first composer's own measurement was that 1/2 onsets at push 1.0 sound and 1/4 onsets do not, so this thins nothing in the notes and moves only the first attack of each run by a sixteenth. 49 onsets were changed.
2. Big descents (more than 4 semitones, not an onset): a further 90 cents down on the commanded tension, 25 notes.
3. Closed-loop corrections: the render is deterministic, so for every note that measured more than 40 cents off I moved its commanded tension by 0.8 times the measured error (floor 59 Hz), then, where that overshot at the low onsets, I split the difference by hand (v4, v5, v6 Eb2, D2, C2, Bb1, B1 onsets) and put the low B1/Bb1 after the octave-moved A2 at 62 Hz.

## Retune (fart_v1)

**What the new instrument did to my parts.** The bass pair (v5, v6) went first. In the first render on fart_v1 (cached tensions, feeding beans*6 at four points), v5 had 33/116 in tune with 40 silent and a mean of 461.1 cents; v6 had 33/100, 17 silent, mean 261.5. Their losses were different: a 1/4 onset at 177 to 200 Hz after a rest, and a B1 or Bb1 at 62 Hz after a jump from A2, measured an octave-ish off (+502 cents in v4 line 3).

**What I checked.** The probes first. `probe --anatomy bass` at push 0.7, cheeks 0.4, tone 0.3 gave 60.0 Hz to 58.1 (-56 cents), 68.9 to 68.2 (-18), 79.2 to 79.3 (+4), 104.5 to 104.8 (+5), 120.0 to 119.1 (-13). Its `--push`, `--cheeks` and `--tone` flags have no effect in `--score` mode (push 0.7 and 1.0 gave identical rows), and the difference comes from the house direction (rasp and jitter), so the score-mode probe does not describe the bass at my settings. Silent notes got tone 0.4 (the valve swung at tone 0.4 where it did not at 0.3).
3. A last pass on the rendered result: notes more than 300 cents off got push 1.0 (5 notes). This changed nothing measurable in v5 (its one note was already at push 1.0; the render was cached), and the B1 and Bb1 at 62 Hz did not move (see below).
