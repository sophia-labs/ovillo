Bars 5–12 introduce that idea and its harmonic replies; bars 13–20 turn toward longer descending phrases and the explicit Ab of Fm6. The middle (21–28) leaves four complete bars silent and answers the piano briefly in the others. Bars 29–36 lift the familiar cell toward E5 and vary its starting point; bars 37–44 recombine descending and ascending shapes rather than copy the first strain. The coda narrows to C5, with a sixteenth D–C turn and B–C wink in bar 47; bar 48 leaves three beats without new lead touches.

Every pitched event is a two-string unison course. Frets range from open to 12; individual events require no spread. Hammer-ons and pull-offs connect immediately adjacent events on the same course. Two slides distinguish the return: G4–A4 in bar 29 and E5–D5 in bar 37. Dynamics are attached to individual gestures from mp through f.
