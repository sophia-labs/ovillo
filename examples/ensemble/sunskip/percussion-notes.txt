Sunskip pocket — invented passive modal percussion

This is a design hypothesis, not measured drum anatomy. Six source cells use
canonical tab IDs only to reach the existing finite boundary pulse. Their
inharmonic spectra, high positive losses, and reciprocal shell couplings are
invented parameters. The three box bodies are shared resonators; no separate
noise oscillator, recording, alternate renderer, or new constitutive law exists.

replaces each source direction, aperture frequency/damping, and palm loss.
